## Ground rules from the brief (apply to every file)
- No invented facts: no fake market stats, prices, company names, contact details, or regulations. Flag anything needing external/legal verification.
- Break vague tasks ("improve SEO", "manage social media") into specific, ordered actions.
- Every service file uses the A–I structure from §4 (Definition, Outcomes, Client Inputs, Task Breakdown table, Deliverables, KPIs, Tools, Risks, SOP).
- Separate one-time vs. recurring (daily/weekly/monthly/quarterly) work.
- Separate agency responsibilities vs. client responsibilities.
- Cross-link related files.
- Named prospect data (real companies) is never fabricated — only the research method/database structure is provided unless real data is supplied.

## commercial/ file ground rules (specific to the 2026-07-24 pricing/value architecture request)
- No A/B testing plans, pricing experiments, customer surveys about pricing, price-sensitivity tests, validation tests, tier-name tests, website conversion tests, or any other testing framework — this is about constructing the value/pricing architecture itself, not testing it.
- Three tiers only: Foundation (DIY), Guided Growth (done-with-you), Managed Growth (done-for-you) — consistent naming across all 13 files.
- No invented Ethiopian birr prices/exact figures — use formulas, adjustable assumptions, and Low/Medium/High/Very High where precise data is unavailable, same discipline as market-mapping/02.
- No fake discounts, false scarcity, inflated reference prices, hidden fees, unusable decoy packages, misleading guarantees, or manipulative choice architecture — every tier must be genuinely deliverable and independently valuable, not just there to push the recommended one.
- Never guarantee rankings, revenue, virality, or other outcomes outside the agency's direct control.
- Explicitly separate: activity → deliverable → immediate output → customer outcome → economic value (per service), and separate advertising spend from agency fees.
