# Service Catalogue

## Purpose of this file

This is the complete list of services the agency can offer, organized by category. Each row links to a detailed service file in `services/` (built per file, per [../job.md](../job.md)) that covers the full delivery process. This file answers "what do we sell and why," not "how do we deliver it" — the how lives in each service file's own A–I breakdown (Definition, Outcomes, Client Inputs, Task Breakdown, Deliverables, KPIs, Tools, Risks, SOP).

No service in this catalogue should be sold to a client by default. Use [market-mapping/06_SERVICE_TO_SEGMENT_MATCHING.md](market-mapping/06_SERVICE_TO_SEGMENT_MATCHING.md) to match services to the specific segment and business maturity, and [commercial/01_SERVICE_PACKAGES.md](commercial/01_SERVICE_PACKAGES.md) for how services combine into sellable packages.

## G. Additional and Emerging Services

Services beyond the original set that are important enough in the Ethiopian and general SME context to be offered or at least evaluated, consolidated in one file so the numbered catalogue above stays stable as new ones are validated:

| Service area | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp Business API / chatbot setup | WhatsApp is a primary inquiry and order channel for many Ethiopian SMEs; catalog, auto-reply, and quick-reply setup is a distinct, sellable service from general WhatsApp marketing |
| E-commerce enablement (online store, catalog, delivery/payment integration) | Distinct from a marketing website — needed by retail, food, and product-based segments moving toward online ordering |
| Marketplace and delivery-platform listing management | Businesses listed on third-party ordering/delivery or classifieds platforms need those listings optimized and monitored, separate from the business's own website |
| Translation and localization (Amharic/English/other local languages) | Underlies nearly every content and website service in a multilingual market; significant enough to be a standalone deliverable and QA checkpoint |
| Print and offline collateral tied to digital campaigns | Menus, table cards, flyers, and signage that carry QR codes, review links, or social handles connect offline foot traffic to digital assets |
| Influencer and creator partnerships | A distinct sourcing, vetting, briefing, and compliance process from paid advertising or organic content |
| Crisis communication and reputation-incident response | A defined escalation process for reputation emergencies (health/safety incidents, viral complaints) beyond routine review response |
| Employer branding and recruitment marketing | Some clients need to market to job candidates, not only customers, using the same social/content skillset |
| Digital-skills training for client staff | Some clients want their own staff trained to handle basic tasks (posting, replying to reviews) rather than fully outsourcing — a consulting/training service in its own right |
| Voice search and structured-data readiness | An emerging extension of technical and local SEO worth tracking as adoption grows |

Full detail for validated items in this group lives in [services/26_ADDITIONAL_SERVICES.md](services/26_ADDITIONAL_SERVICES.md). Do not sell an item from this table until it has been written up there with the same rigor as the numbered services.

## How Services Combine

No client should be sold services 1–26 individually by default. Services combine into the packages defined in [commercial/01_SERVICE_PACKAGES.md](commercial/01_SERVICE_PACKAGES.md):

- **Digital Foundation** — mainly services 10, 11, 12, 25 (basic)
- **Local Visibility** — mainly services 6, 9, 10, 11, 1–5 (basic site or landing page)
- **Social Growth** — mainly services 12–16
- **Lead Generation** — mainly services 1–5, 17, 18, 20, 21, 23
- **Full Digital Growth** — most of A–F combined
- **B2B Growth** — mainly services 1–5 (corporate site), 12 (LinkedIn), 20, 22, 23

See the package file for exact scope, exclusions, and pricing logic per package.

## Standard Operating Procedure — Maintaining This Catalogue

- [ ] Do not add a numbered service until its full file exists in `services/` with the A–I structure.
- [ ] When a new service idea is validated (client demand confirmed, delivery process defined, pricing logic exists), move it from Section G into a numbered entry and give it its own `services/` file.
- [ ] Review this catalogue whenever `01_AGENCY_STRATEGY_AND_POSITIONING.md` §10 (service selection philosophy) changes.
- [ ] Keep every "primary outcome" phrased as a business result, not an activity (e.g. "more calls," not "improved visibility").
