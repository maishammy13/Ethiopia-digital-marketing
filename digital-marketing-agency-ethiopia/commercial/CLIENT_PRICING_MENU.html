Common questions

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?+

Foundation is a one-time project with no ongoing commitment. Guided Growth and Managed Growth have a 3-month minimum so we have time to produce real results, then continue month to month after that.

I don't have a website yet. Can I still start?+

Yes. All three packages can include building your website from scratch as part of the setup.

Can I move to a different tier later?+

Yes. Most clients start with Foundation or Guided Growth and move up once they're ready for us to take on more.

Does the monthly fee include advertising spend?+

No. Any ad budget for Facebook, Instagram, Google, or TikTok is paid directly to that platform and is separate from our management fee.

How soon can we get started?+

Usually within a few business days of your discovery call, once we've agreed the scope and you've shared access to the accounts we'll need.

What if I'm not happy with the work?+

Guided Growth and Managed Growth include a set number of revision rounds on major deliverables. If something still isn't right, tell us. We would rather fix it than have you stay quiet and leave.

Do you work with businesses outside Addis Ababa?+

Yes. Most of our work happens remotely regardless of location. Anything that needs an in-person visit, like a photography shoot, is scoped and quoted separately based on where you are.

What do you need from me to get started?+

Access to the relevant accounts (your Google Business Profile, social accounts, website if you have one), basic business information, and a bit of your time for the initial discovery call. We'll send a simple checklist once you sign on.

Who actually owns my website and accounts?+

You do. Your domain, hosting, Google Business Profile, and social media accounts stay in your name. We work inside them with access you grant us, and that access can be removed at any time.

Is my business information kept confidential?+

Yes. Anything you share with us, including account access and business data, is used only to deliver your work and is not shared with anyone else.

Can I add extra services later?+

Yes. Most clients start with one or two priorities and add services like paid advertising or a dedicated photo shoot once the foundation is in place.
