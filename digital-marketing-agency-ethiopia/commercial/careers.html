Interested in working with Sigma?

We're a small, growing agency. We don't always have open roles, but we're always interested in hearing from people who do good work.

Why work with us

We recommend the smallest solution that actually works for a client, not the biggest one we can sell. If that approach to the work matters to you as much as it matters to us, we're probably a good fit for each other.

We're small enough that everyone's work is visible and everyone's judgment matters. We're also honest with clients about what we can and can't guarantee, and we expect the same honesty internally.
