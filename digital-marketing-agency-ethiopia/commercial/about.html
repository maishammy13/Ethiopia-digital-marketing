About Sigma

A digital marketing agency built around one idea: the smallest solution that actually works.

We would rather set up your Google listing properly than sell you a website you don't need yet.

Why we focus on Ethiopian small and medium businesses

Most digital marketing advice is written for large companies with big budgets and full-time marketing teams. Ethiopian small and medium businesses need something different: a right-sized plan, delivered honestly, at a price that makes sense for a business their size.

We work with restaurants, clinics, hotels, salons, retailers, and schools across Ethiopia. Businesses run by owners who are busy running the business, not managing a marketing department.

How we work

We recommend the smallest package that produces a real, measurable result, then grow from there as it proves itself. We never guarantee outcomes we don't control, like a specific ranking, follower count, or revenue figure. We do guarantee the deliverables we're responsible for: a working website, a verified Google listing, a report that tells you the truth about what happened that month.

You own your own website, your own Google Business Profile, and your own social media accounts. We work inside them with the access you grant us, not the other way around.

What we won't do

No fake reviews

We never buy, write, or gate reviews. Every customer is asked the same way, whether the last interaction went well or not.

No guaranteed rankings or revenue

Anyone who promises a specific search ranking or revenue number is promising something outside their control. We won't do that.

No locked-in accounts

Your domain, hosting, Google listing, and social accounts stay in your name and under your control, always.

No invented results

If we don't have real proof yet, we say so. That's why our case studies page is still empty. We would rather be honest than convincing.
