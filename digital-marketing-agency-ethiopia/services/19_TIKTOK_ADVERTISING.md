# Service: TikTok Advertising

## A. Service Definition

**What it is.** Planning, setup, execution, and ongoing management of paid campaigns on TikTok via TikTok Ads Manager, using native, short-form video creative to reach TikTok's audience — including boosting strong organic posts through Spark Ads.

**What business problem it solves.** TikTok's audience and content culture are video-native and entertainment-first. A business that simply reposts its Instagram or Facebook ad creative to TikTok typically underperforms, because it reads as an obvious, unnatural ad rather than native platform content. This service builds and manages campaigns specifically for how TikTok actually works.

**Why a customer would pay for it.** TikTok ad creative needs to feel native to the platform rather than overtly promotional, and campaign setup, targeting, and tracking require platform-specific skill distinct from Meta or Google. Done well, TikTok can reach an audience — often younger and highly engaged — that other platforms don't reach as effectively.

**Business types that benefit most.** Businesses whose real target customer includes a meaningful TikTok-native audience, and who can produce or commission genuinely native-feeling short video content — see the "Social Media" importance and visual-content-suitability ratings in [../market-mapping/06_SERVICE_TO_SEGMENT_MATCHING.md](../market-mapping/06_SERVICE_TO_SEGMENT_MATCHING.md)/[../market-mapping/02_CUSTOMER_SEGMENT_PRIORITIZATION.md](../market-mapping/02_CUSTOMER_SEGMENT_PRIORITIZATION.md) (fashion/beauty, food and beverage, entertainment, fitness, and education aimed at a younger audience). **This is not a default recommendation for every client** — see Task 1.1.

**What this does not include.** Organic TikTok account management ([13_SOCIAL_MEDIA_MANAGEMENT.md](13_SOCIAL_MEDIA_MANAGEMENT.md)), though this service coordinates with it for Spark Ads. Video production of the underlying content itself ([15_CONTENT_PRODUCTION.md](15_CONTENT_PRODUCTION.md)/[16_PHOTOGRAPHY_AND_VIDEO.md](16_PHOTOGRAPHY_AND_VIDEO.md) — this service defines the TikTok-specific creative brief, it doesn't replace those production services). Other advertising platforms (17/18).

**How it connects to other services.** Uses assets and coordinates a TikTok-specific brief with 15/16. Boosts high-performing organic TikTok posts flagged by 13 via Spark Ads. Depends on the TikTok Pixel/Events API installed alongside other tracking under [03_WEBSITE_DEVELOPMENT.md](03_WEBSITE_DEVELOPMENT.md)/[24_ANALYTICS_TRACKING_AND_REPORTING.md](24_ANALYTICS_TRACKING_AND_REPORTING.md). Routes leads into [23_CRM_AND_MARKETING_AUTOMATION.md](23_CRM_AND_MARKETING_AUTOMATION.md).

## B. Desired Business Outcomes

| Level | Example |
|---|---|
| Activity | Audience-fit assessment, campaign strategy and setup, native creative briefing, targeting, tracking setup, ongoing optimization |
| Output | Live campaigns, native video ad creative, Spark Ads boosts, performance reports |
| Outcome | Reach, engagement, traffic, or leads among TikTok's audience at a measured cost |
| Financial impact | Cost per lead/return on ad spend, evaluated including whether this reach is genuinely incremental to what other platforms already deliver |

## C. Inputs Required From the Client

- TikTok Ads Manager account, owned by the client, with a client-controlled payment method
- TikTok Pixel/Events API installed and verified
- Budget and campaign objective(s)
- Native video creative, or agreement to produce it via 15/16 with a TikTok-specific brief
- The organic TikTok account from 12/13, if Spark Ads are in scope
- The landing page or destination traffic will be sent to
- Target-audience detail from personas
- Awareness of any advertising restrictions applicable to the client's sector

## H. Risks and Common Failure Modes

- **Technical risk:** a broken Pixel/Events API silently producing inaccurate optimization and reporting data.
- **Platform risk:** TikTok's ad policies, formats, and trending styles change quickly — verify current guidance before each campaign cycle.
- **Client-related risk:** a client insisting on reusing polished, non-native creative from another platform against the platform-fit evidence.
- **Data and access risk:** running campaigns through an agency-owned account instead of the client's.
- **Reputation risk:** creative that feels overtly promotional or out of step with platform culture tends to underperform and can reflect poorly on the brand.
- **Security risk:** account access should follow the same role-based, minimum-necessary principle as other platform access.
- **Legal or compliance risk:** ⚠️-flagged sectors face specific TikTok advertising restrictions that must be checked before any campaign in those categories.
- **Measurement and attribution risk:** platform-reported conversions can diverge from independently verified outcomes; report the gap honestly, and be candid about the difficulty of measuring true incrementality.
