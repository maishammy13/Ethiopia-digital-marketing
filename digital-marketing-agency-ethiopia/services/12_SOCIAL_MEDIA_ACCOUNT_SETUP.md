# Service: Social Media Account Setup

## A. Service Definition

**What it is.** Selecting the right platforms for a specific business and creating properly configured, secure, professional social media accounts (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, Telegram, or others, as relevant) under the client's own ownership — ready to hand off to ongoing management.

**What business problem it solves.** Many SME social accounts started as a personal profile converted awkwardly into a business page, are missing key business information and links, have no security protections, or exist on platforms the target customer barely uses while the platform they actually use is neglected.

**Why a customer would pay for it.** Correct account type, category, and setup affects discoverability, analytics access, advertising eligibility, and first-impression credibility from day one. Picking the right platforms in the first place avoids wasting management effort on channels with little reach to real customers.

**Business types that benefit most.** Any business establishing a new social presence, rebranding, or currently operating on a personal profile instead of a proper business account. Platform choice varies enormously by segment — see the "Social Media" ratings in [../market-mapping/06_SERVICE_TO_SEGMENT_MATCHING.md](../market-mapping/06_SERVICE_TO_SEGMENT_MATCHING.md) (e.g. visually strong consumer segments benefit heavily from Instagram/TikTok, B2B/professional segments benefit more from LinkedIn, and not every business needs every platform).

**What this does not include.** Ongoing posting, community management, content production, or performance optimization — that is [13_SOCIAL_MEDIA_MANAGEMENT.md](13_SOCIAL_MEDIA_MANAGEMENT.md). Actual content creation is [15_CONTENT_PRODUCTION.md](15_CONTENT_PRODUCTION.md). Paid-advertising account configuration beyond basic business-account eligibility is covered in the relevant advertising service (17/18/19).

**How it connects to other services.** A direct prerequisite for 13. Uses brand assets from [25_BRANDING_AND_GRAPHIC_DESIGN.md](25_BRANDING_AND_GRAPHIC_DESIGN.md) and bio/description copy consistent with [04_WEBSITE_COPYWRITING.md](04_WEBSITE_COPYWRITING.md). Informed by personas from [01_WEBSITE_STRATEGY.md](01_WEBSITE_STRATEGY.md)/[../market-mapping/04_BUYER_PERSONAS.md](../market-mapping/04_BUYER_PERSONAS.md). The accounts created here are the accounts eventually connected to advertising in 17/18/19.

## B. Desired Business Outcomes

| Level | Example |
|---|---|
| Activity | Platform selection, account creation/conversion, profile completion, security configuration |
| Output | Live, secure, fully completed social profiles on the right platforms, under client ownership, with a defined admin-role structure |
| Outcome | A professional, credible, discoverable social presence specifically on the platforms the business's real customers actually use |
| Financial impact | Avoided wasted future management effort on the wrong platforms; a secure foundation that avoids the cost and disruption of a lost or compromised account |

## C. Inputs Required From the Client

- Brand assets (logo, colors) from [25_BRANDING_AND_GRAPHIC_DESIGN.md](25_BRANDING_AND_GRAPHIC_DESIGN.md), or agreement to use placeholders pending that service
- Business description/bio content, or draft input for it
- Contact information: phone, WhatsApp number, email, website
- Business category
- A business email address and phone number the client controls, for account verification and recovery
- Target-customer information from strategy/personas, to inform platform selection
- Access to any existing social accounts, including any personal-profile-as-business-page situation
- A list of staff who should have admin/manager access, and at what role level

## D. Detailed Task Breakdown

### 1. Preparation

**Task: Confirm verification details and account-ownership plan**
| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Prevents the common failure of creating accounts under a staff member's or the agency's personal credentials instead of the client's own control |
| Inputs | Client's business email, phone number |
| Procedure | 1) Confirm a business (not personal) email and phone number the client controls will be used for every account. 2) Confirm every account will be created under the client's ownership, with the agency granted delegated admin access, per [../operations/14_SECURITY_AND_ACCESS_MANAGEMENT.md](../operations/14_SECURITY_AND_ACCESS_MANAGEMENT.md). |
| Tools | Discovery conversation |
| Deliverable | Confirmed ownership and verification plan |
| Owner | Social media specialist |
| Dependency | Onboarding complete |
| Frequency | One-time per client |
| KPI | Ownership plan confirmed before any account is created |
| Quality check | Confirmed the email/phone used is one the client will retain control of long-term, not a personal staff account |
| Common mistake | Creating accounts under an employee's personal phone number, causing an access crisis if that employee later leaves |
| Estimated complexity | Low |

### 2. Research

**Task: Audit existing accounts**
| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Reveals whether an account already exists (possibly with existing followers/history worth preserving) before creating a duplicate |
| Inputs | Business name, known handles |
| Procedure | 1) Search each relevant platform for any existing account under the business's name or close variations. 2) For any found, assess whether to reclaim/clean up the existing account (preserving history) or start fresh. 3) Check for impersonating or outdated duplicate accounts. |
| Tools | Manual platform search |
| Deliverable | Existing-account audit and reclaim/fresh-start decision per platform |
| Owner | Social media specialist |
| Dependency | Task 1.1 |
| Frequency | One-time per client |
| KPI | Search completed on every platform in scope before proceeding |
| Quality check | Decision to abandon an existing account (rather than reclaim it) justified in writing if it has meaningful existing followers/history |
| Common mistake | Creating a brand-new account without checking for an existing one, losing accumulated followers and history unnecessarily |
| Estimated complexity | Low |

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Not every business needs every platform — effort should go where the real target customer actually spends time |

| Owner | Social media specialist |

| Tools | Platform username-availability checks |

| Owner | Social media specialist |
| Dependency | Task 4.1 |

## H. Risks and Common Failure Modes

- **Technical risk:** incorrect image sizing causing a cropped or blurry profile/cover image.
- **Platform risk:** platform account-creation and verification requirements change over time — verify current requirements at the time of setup.
- **Client-related risk:** a client wanting accounts created under a personal staff member's credentials "for simplicity," creating an ownership risk (see [../operations/14_SECURITY_AND_ACCESS_MANAGEMENT.md](../operations/14_SECURITY_AND_ACCESS_MANAGEMENT.md)).
- **Data and access risk:** losing access to a newly created account if recovery information wasn't properly documented and verified.
- **Reputation risk:** an incomplete, inconsistent, or clearly abandoned-looking profile undermines trust the moment a prospective customer visits it.
- **Security risk:** skipping two-factor authentication is the single most common, most avoidable cause of account hijacking.
- **Legal or compliance risk:** business-category and bio claims for ⚠️-flagged sectors must respect the same restrictions noted in [../operations/15_LEGAL_COMPLIANCE_AND_RISK.md](../operations/15_LEGAL_COMPLIANCE_AND_RISK.md).
- **Measurement and attribution risk:** early follower or engagement numbers reflect the setup's quality only marginally — most of that outcome depends on the ongoing content and management work in 13.

## I. Standard Operating Procedure

- [ ] Verification email/phone and ownership plan confirmed before any account is created
- [ ] Existing accounts audited; reclaim-vs-fresh-start decision made per platform
- [ ] Platform selection made and documented with reasoning tied to the real target customer
- [ ] Account structure and admin roles defined at minimum-necessary access levels
- [ ] Accounts created/converted as proper business accounts with the correct category
- [ ] Consistent usernames secured across all selected platforms
- [ ] Profile image, cover image, and bio prepared and correctly sized per platform
- [ ] Contact buttons, links, and business information fields fully configured and tested
- [ ] Two-factor authentication enabled and recovery information documented on every account
- [ ] All links/buttons/displayed details verified on a real mobile device
- [ ] Profiles made public and client sign-off obtained
- [ ] Early access/verification issues monitored and resolved in the first week
- [ ] Setup completion report delivered with secure access handover
- [ ] Security settings and information accuracy re-verified quarterly
