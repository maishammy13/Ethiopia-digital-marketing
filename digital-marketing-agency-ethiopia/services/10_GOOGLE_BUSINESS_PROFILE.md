# Service: Google Business Profile

## A. Service Definition

**What it is.** Setup and full ongoing management of a business's free Google Business Profile (GBP) listing — the profile that appears on Google Maps and in local/Map Pack search results. Covers eligibility, verification, every profile field, photos/video, posts, Q&A, review response, performance tracking, and suspension prevention/recovery.

**What business problem it solves.** An unclaimed, incomplete, inconsistent, or unmanaged profile suppresses Maps and local-pack visibility and looks untrustworthy to a customer comparing options. Profiles can also be hijacked, duplicated, or suspended — problems most business owners don't know how to prevent or fix.

**Why a customer would pay for it.** GBP is free to run but has many small, easy-to-get-wrong details (categories, attributes, verification method, posting cadence, suspension triggers) that materially affect visibility. Professional management captures value most SME owners leave on the table simply from not knowing the platform's mechanics.

**Business types that benefit most.** Any business with a physical location or a defined service area — nearly every segment in [../market-mapping/01_ETHIOPIA_CUSTOMER_SEGMENT_MAP.md](../market-mapping/01_ETHIOPIA_CUSTOMER_SEGMENT_MAP.md), and especially the "Very High" GBP-importance rows in [../market-mapping/06_SERVICE_TO_SEGMENT_MATCHING.md](../market-mapping/06_SERVICE_TO_SEGMENT_MATCHING.md) (restaurants, hotels, clinics, salons, gyms, and most other proximity-driven consumer businesses).

**What this does not include.** Citation-building and NAP consistency across the *rest* of the web ([09_LOCAL_SEO.md](09_LOCAL_SEO.md) — this service manages the Google listing itself, treated as one citation source within that broader discipline). The client-facing review-generation system (asking customers for reviews via QR codes, WhatsApp, SMS — [11_REVIEW_AND_REPUTATION_MANAGEMENT.md](11_REVIEW_AND_REPUTATION_MANAGEMENT.md) — though this service does execute the actual on-platform response to reviews). Paid Google advertising, which is a separate product and account ([18_GOOGLE_ADVERTISING.md](18_GOOGLE_ADVERTISING.md)).

**How it connects to other services.** One of three tightly linked local-visibility pillars alongside 09 (Local SEO) and 11 (Reviews) — see the cross-reference in 09's Service Definition. GBP performance data feeds [24_ANALYTICS_TRACKING_AND_REPORTING.md](24_ANALYTICS_TRACKING_AND_REPORTING.md). Photos and business information gathered here are often reused in [17_META_ADVERTISING.md](17_META_ADVERTISING.md)/[18_GOOGLE_ADVERTISING.md](18_GOOGLE_ADVERTISING.md) creative.

### How Google Business Profile relates to adjacent concepts

Clients frequently confuse these — clarify explicitly during sales and onboarding:

| Concept | What it actually is |
|---|---|
| **Google Business Profile** | The free, business-owner-managed listing (name, hours, photos, posts, reviews) that powers what appears on Maps and in the local Map Pack |
| **Google Maps** | The map product itself; GBP is the input, Maps (and Search) is where it's displayed |
| **Local SEO** | The broader discipline of ranking well in local/"near me" search, of which GBP is one major component alongside citations (09) and on-site local signals |
| **Organic SEO** | Ranking in general (non-local, non-paid) search results — a website's own SEO (06/07/08), distinct from the Maps/local-pack result set GBP feeds |
| **Paid Google advertising** | Google Ads — a paid product where the business pays per click/impression; entirely separate account and budget from the free GBP listing (18) |
| **Online reviews** | Customer-submitted ratings/feedback that appear on GBP (and other platforms) and function as a ranking and trust signal; the review-generation process itself lives in 11, review response is managed here |
| **Business directories** | Third-party listing sites (both general and industry-specific) distinct from Google; managed under 09's citation-building work, not this service |

## B. Desired Business Outcomes

| Level | Example |
|---|---|
| Activity | Verification, profile completion, photo/post management, Q&A monitoring, review response, performance monitoring |
| Output | A complete, verified, actively managed GBP profile; regular posts; managed Q&A; timely review responses; monthly performance report |
| Outcome | Higher visibility in Maps and the local Map Pack; more calls, direction requests, messages, bookings, and website clicks directly from the free listing |
| Financial impact | Often the highest-ROI local-marketing channel available, since the platform itself is free — value is created almost entirely through management quality |

## C. Inputs Required From the Client

- Exact legal/trading business name (see Google's name-accuracy rules, Task 1.1)
- Exact address (or, for a service-area business with no public-facing address, the defined service area)
- Telephone number and WhatsApp number
- Website URL
- Category information (what the business actually is/does, in Google's terms)
- Opening hours, including known upcoming special/holiday hours
- Products, services, and prices to the extent the client wants them published
- Photographs, video, logo, and a cover image, or agreement to commission these via [16_PHOTOGRAPHY_AND_VIDEO.md](16_PHOTOGRAPHY_AND_VIDEO.md)
- A Google account the client owns (profile ownership rules, Task 1.1) or explicit delegation of manager-level access to the agency
- Awareness of any past suspension, verification difficulty, or known duplicate listings

| Calls, clicks, messages, bookings, direction requests | Lagging | The most directly meaningful outcome metrics; still influenced by review volume/rating (11) and broader local SEO (09), not this service alone |
| Map/local-pack ranking | Lagging | Shared outcome across the three local-visibility pillars (09, 10, 11) — see the same caution in 09 |

## H. Risks and Common Failure Modes

- **Technical risk:** an incorrectly entered address or service-area configuration can misplace the business on the map entirely.
- **Platform risk:** Google's policies, verification methods, and available features change over time — verify current rules rather than relying on outdated guidance, especially before advising on suspension recovery.
- **Client-related risk:** a client or their staff making unauthorized edits (or allowing an unauthorized public suggestion to be accepted) that reintroduce inaccurate information.
- **Data and access risk:** profile created or managed under the agency's account instead of the client's — see [../operations/14_SECURITY_AND_ACCESS_MANAGEMENT.md](../operations/14_SECURITY_AND_ACCESS_MANAGEMENT.md).
- **Reputation risk:** an unanswered negative review or an unmonitored Q&A section left with a wrong public answer.
- **Security risk:** profile access should be granted at the appropriate role level and revoked immediately for departing staff (see [../operations/14_SECURITY_AND_ACCESS_MANAGEMENT.md](../operations/14_SECURITY_AND_ACCESS_MANAGEMENT.md)).
- **Legal or compliance risk:** for ⚠️-flagged sectors, category and description content must not imply unverified credentials or claims; never fabricate or incentivize reviews (see [11_REVIEW_AND_REPUTATION_MANAGEMENT.md](11_REVIEW_AND_REPUTATION_MANAGEMENT.md)).
- **Measurement and attribution risk:** GBP-reported "calls" and "clicks" are the platform's own counts and may not perfectly reconcile with independent analytics — report them as the platform's own figures, not claimed as independently verified.
