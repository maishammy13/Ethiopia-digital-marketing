# Service: Content Production

## A. Service Definition

**What it is.** The actual creation of content assets: developing specific ideas from [14_CONTENT_STRATEGY.md](14_CONTENT_STRATEGY.md)'s topic bank into finished, publish-ready posts — scriptwriting, coordinating photography and video capture, editing, caption writing, hashtag/keyword research, and securing client approval before handoff to [13_SOCIAL_MEDIA_MANAGEMENT.md](13_SOCIAL_MEDIA_MANAGEMENT.md) for scheduling.

**What business problem it solves.** A strategy and calendar mean nothing without execution. Poor-quality production — blurry photos, generic captions, inconsistent editing — undermines even a well-planned pillar and objective structure, and generic or stock-feeling content is easy for a target customer to scroll past.

**Why a customer would pay for it.** Consistent, professional-quality content requires photography, video, writing, and editing skill and time most business owners don't have. Authentic, well-produced content genuinely outperforms generic or low-effort posts in engagement and trust.

**Business types that benefit most.** Any business with a content strategy (14) and management (13) in place, especially visually strong segments — see the "Visual-content suitability" dimension in [../market-mapping/02_CUSTOMER_SEGMENT_PRIORITIZATION.md](../market-mapping/02_CUSTOMER_SEGMENT_PRIORITIZATION.md).

**What this does not include.** Content pillar/objective/calendar strategy ([14_CONTENT_STRATEGY.md](14_CONTENT_STRATEGY.md)), scheduling/publishing/community management ([13_SOCIAL_MEDIA_MANAGEMENT.md](13_SOCIAL_MEDIA_MANAGEMENT.md)), or larger dedicated productions like a full brand photo/video shoot — those are scoped and delivered as their own engagement under [16_PHOTOGRAPHY_AND_VIDEO.md](16_PHOTOGRAPHY_AND_VIDEO.md), with this service handling the lighter, ongoing content capture that fits a regular posting cadence.

**How it connects to other services.** Consumes the calendar and topic bank from 14. Hands off finished assets to 13. Coordinates with 16 for larger planned shoots and with [25_BRANDING_AND_GRAPHIC_DESIGN.md](25_BRANDING_AND_GRAPHIC_DESIGN.md) for visual consistency. Hashtag/keyword research here overlaps with and should stay consistent with [07_ON_PAGE_SEO.md](07_ON_PAGE_SEO.md)'s keyword work where relevant.

## B. Desired Business Outcomes

| Level | Example |
|---|---|
| Activity | Scriptwriting, capturing photo/video, editing, caption writing, hashtag research, securing client approval |
| Output | Finished, publish-ready content assets |
| Outcome | Consistent, professional-quality content that genuinely fulfills the strategy's pillars and objectives |
| Financial impact | Content that engages and converts rather than being generic filler; efficient turnaround that avoids production bottlenecks breaking the publishing calendar |

## C. Inputs Required From the Client

- The current content calendar and topic bank from 14
- Brand voice and visual guidelines from 25
- Access to the filming location, products, staff, or subjects needed per the calendar
- Any existing photo/video assets from 16 available for reuse
- A defined approval process and realistic turnaround expectation
- Relevant hashtag/keyword input, coordinated with 07/14

## D. Detailed Task Breakdown

### 1. Preparation

**Task: Review the period's calendar and confirm asset/access needs**
| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Identifies exactly what needs to be filmed, photographed, or written for each calendar item before production begins, and flags any access dependency early |
| Inputs | Approved calendar from 14 |
| Procedure | 1) Go through each calendar item and determine whether it needs new photography/video, can reuse an existing asset, or is text/graphic-only. 2) Identify any access needed (a location visit, staff availability, a specific product) and confirm timing with the client. |
| Tools | Calendar, shared document |
| Deliverable | Production needs list per calendar item |
| Owner | Content producer |
| Dependency | 14, Task 8.1 (approved calendar) |
| Frequency | Per planning cycle |
| KPI | Needs list completed before the cycle's production work begins |
| Quality check | Every calendar item has a clear, resolved production plan, with no "figure it out later" items |
| Common mistake | Discovering a needed location or staff access isn't actually available only on the planned filming day, losing the slot |
| Estimated complexity | Low |

### 2. Research

**Task: Hashtag and keyword research per platform**
| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Relevant, well-researched hashtags and keywords improve discoverability beyond a business's existing followers |
| Inputs | Content pillars, platform norms |
| Procedure | 1) Research relevant, actively used hashtags per platform and pillar (avoiding banned, overused, or irrelevant tags). 2) Cross-check for consistency with keyword themes from [07_ON_PAGE_SEO.md](07_ON_PAGE_SEO.md) where the content and website target overlapping topics. |
| Tools | Native platform search/suggestion tools, manual research |
| Deliverable | Hashtag/keyword bank per pillar and platform |
| Owner | Content producer |
| Dependency | 14's pillar framework |
| Frequency | One-time initial bank, refreshed quarterly |
| KPI | Every pillar has a relevant, current hashtag/keyword set |
| Quality check | Hashtags checked for current relevance — some become stale, banned, or spammy-associated over time |
| Common mistake | Reusing the exact same hashtag set on every post regardless of topic, reducing relevance and looking repetitive |

### 3. Strategy

| Purpose | A short, clear script or talking-point outline produces a far more effective video than unscripted filming |
| Inputs | Content brief |

| Deliverable | Video scripts/talking-point outlines |
| Owner | Content producer |
| Dependency | Task 3.1 |
| Frequency | Per video item |
| KPI | Every video item has a script or outline before filming |
| Quality check | Script reviewed for length — most short-form video needs a tight, front-loaded message |
| Common mistake | Filming without any script or outline, producing rambling footage that's difficult to edit into something focused |
| Estimated complexity | Medium |

**Task: Capture photography and video**

|---|---|
| Purpose | Produces the raw visual material the finished content is built from |

| Frequency | Per planning cycle |

| Common mistake | Leaving the session without reviewing captured material, only discovering a focus or audio problem during editing when it's too late to reshoot easily |

## H. Risks and Common Failure Modes

- **Technical risk:** poor lighting, audio, or focus discovered only after leaving the filming location.
- **Platform risk:** editing to the wrong aspect ratio or length for a given platform's current format requirements.
- **Client-related risk:** slow client approval turnaround creating a bottleneck that risks missing the posting calendar — flag this pattern in reporting rather than silently absorbing the delay.
- **Data and access risk:** losing raw footage/photos due to inconsistent storage — maintain the organized library discipline in Task 12.1.
- **Reputation risk:** inconsistent visual style or a factual error in a caption undermines the professional impression the whole content strategy is meant to build.
- **Security risk:** not typically applicable directly to this service, beyond standard access controls on shared storage.
- **Legal or compliance risk:** captions and visuals for ⚠️-flagged sectors must respect the same claim restrictions as copywriting; any people appearing in photos/video need appropriate consent.
- **Measurement and attribution risk:** engagement performance reflects strategy and management execution as much as production quality — avoid claiming sole credit for a strong-performing post.

## I. Standard Operating Procedure

- [ ] Period's calendar reviewed; production needs and access confirmed per item
- [ ] Hashtag/keyword bank current for every pillar and platform in scope
- [ ] Content briefs written for every scheduled item before production begins
- [ ] Filming/photography sessions scheduled and batched efficiently, equipment checked
- [ ] Scripts/talking-point outlines written for every video item
- [ ] Photo/video captured with sufficient material and reviewed on-site
- [ ] Assets edited to platform-appropriate format and brand-consistent style
- [ ] Captions written matching the brief, brand voice, and including relevant hashtags/keywords
- [ ] Internal quality review completed before client approval is requested
- [ ] Client approval obtained within the agreed turnaround; handoff to 13 confirmed complete
- [ ] Production turnaround and backlog tracked weekly against the posting cadence
- [ ] Repurposing candidates identified monthly; accuracy re-checked before reposting
- [ ] Production approach refined quarterly based on performance evidence
- [ ] Content-asset library kept organized and locatable for future repurposing
