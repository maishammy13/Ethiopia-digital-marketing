# Service: Photography and Video

## A. Service Definition

**What it is.** Dedicated, planned photography and video production — larger shoots that produce a reusable library of high-quality visual assets (product photography, interior/exterior location photography, staff/team photos, brand video, testimonial video, promotional video) used across the website, Google Business Profile, social media, and advertising.

**What business problem it solves.** Authentic, professional visual assets are foundational to nearly every other service this agency delivers. Without them, businesses fall back on generic stock photography or low-quality phone snapshots, both of which quietly undermine the trust every other service is trying to build.

**Why a customer would pay for it.** Professional photography and video require equipment, lighting, and skill most businesses don't have in-house. A well-planned shoot produces a reusable asset library covering months of downstream content needs efficiently, rather than scrambling for images piecemeal.

**Business types that benefit most.** Any business, but especially visually strong segments — see "Visual-content suitability" in [../market-mapping/02_CUSTOMER_SEGMENT_PRIORITIZATION.md](../market-mapping/02_CUSTOMER_SEGMENT_PRIORITIZATION.md) (restaurants, hotels, beauty, retail, real estate, events, and most consumer-facing segments with a physical presence).

**What this does not include.** Ongoing, lightweight content capture for the regular posting cadence — that is [15_CONTENT_PRODUCTION.md](15_CONTENT_PRODUCTION.md), which this service supplies raw source material to but does not replace. Editing performed specifically for an advertising creative variant belongs to the relevant advertising service (17/18/19), though this service can supply the underlying footage/photos. Logo and graphic design work is [25_BRANDING_AND_GRAPHIC_DESIGN.md](25_BRANDING_AND_GRAPHIC_DESIGN.md).

**How it connects to other services.** Supplies the asset library used by [02_WEBSITE_DESIGN.md](02_WEBSITE_DESIGN.md)/[03_WEBSITE_DEVELOPMENT.md](03_WEBSITE_DEVELOPMENT.md), [10_GOOGLE_BUSINESS_PROFILE.md](10_GOOGLE_BUSINESS_PROFILE.md), [12_SOCIAL_MEDIA_ACCOUNT_SETUP.md](12_SOCIAL_MEDIA_ACCOUNT_SETUP.md)/[15_CONTENT_PRODUCTION.md](15_CONTENT_PRODUCTION.md), and [17_META_ADVERTISING.md](17_META_ADVERTISING.md)/[18_GOOGLE_ADVERTISING.md](18_GOOGLE_ADVERTISING.md)/[19_TIKTOK_ADVERTISING.md](19_TIKTOK_ADVERTISING.md). Follows brand guidelines from [25_BRANDING_AND_GRAPHIC_DESIGN.md](25_BRANDING_AND_GRAPHIC_DESIGN.md).

## B. Desired Business Outcomes

| Level | Example |
|---|---|
| Activity | Shoot planning, on-location photography/video capture, editing and post-production, asset delivery |
| Output | An organized, licensed, ready-to-use asset library covering photos and video in the formats/crops each downstream service needs |
| Outcome | Every other service has authentic, professional, on-brand visual material to work with instead of generic stock imagery |
| Financial impact | Improved trust and conversion from real imagery versus stock; an efficient one-time investment that covers many months of downstream content needs across multiple services |

## C. Inputs Required From the Client

- Brand guidelines from 25
- Consolidated shot-list needs from every service that will use the output (website pages, GBP, social pillars, ad creative)
- Access to the location, products, staff, and (with consent) real customers to be photographed/filmed
- Any timing constraints (e.g. must shoot during specific business hours, around a live event)
- Signed model/consent releases for any identifiable people appearing in the material
- A realistic budget and timeline for the shoot, including any specialist equipment needs (e.g. drone footage, requiring separate authorization/permits where applicable)

## D. Detailed Task Breakdown

### 1. Preparation

**Task: Consolidate cross-service shot-list needs**
| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

| Inputs | Requirements from 02/03, 10, 12/15, and 17/18/19 |
| Procedure | 1) Collect specific visual needs from each relevant service (e.g. website needs a hero image and team photo; GBP needs exterior/interior/product shots; social needs vertical video; ads need a specific aspect ratio). 2) Consolidate into one master shot list. |

| Owner | Photography/video specialist |

| Frequency | One-time per shoot, refreshed for each subsequent shoot |

| Quality check | List reviewed with each requesting service before finalizing |
| Common mistake | Planning a shoot around only the most vocal service's needs (e.g. website) and missing what social or advertising will separately need, requiring a costly reshoot |
| Estimated complexity | Medium |

**Task: Confirm consent and release process**
| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Using someone's image without proper consent creates real legal and reputational risk |
| Inputs | Anticipated subjects (staff, customers) |
| Procedure | 1) Prepare a simple model/consent release form. 2) Confirm the process for obtaining signed consent from any staff or customer who will be identifiably photographed or filmed. |

| Owner | Photography/video specialist, account lead |

| Frequency | One-time process setup, used per shoot |

| Quality check | Release form reviewed for clarity and completeness |

## H. Risks and Common Failure Modes

- **Technical risk:** poor lighting or audio discovered only after the shoot has ended, requiring a costly reshoot.
- **Platform risk:** delivering assets in formats that don't match what downstream platforms actually require, causing rework.
- **Client-related risk:** a subject, prop, or location not being available as planned on the shoot day.
- **Data and access risk:** losing raw or edited files due to inconsistent storage — maintain the same discipline as [15_CONTENT_PRODUCTION.md](15_CONTENT_PRODUCTION.md) Task 12.1.
- **Reputation risk:** using an outdated asset (old menu, departed staff member) undermines trust once a customer notices the discrepancy in person.
- **Security risk:** not typically applicable directly, beyond standard access controls on shared storage.
- **Legal or compliance risk:** using an identifiable person's image without signed consent is a genuine legal exposure, not just a best practice — this must never be skipped, regardless of time pressure on the shoot day.
- **Measurement and attribution risk:** the impact of better imagery is real but difficult to isolate precisely from other concurrent changes — avoid overstating a specific, precisely measured conversion lift without genuine controlled evidence.

## I. Standard Operating Procedure

- [ ] Cross-service shot-list needs consolidated into one master list before planning begins
- [ ] Consent/release process ready before the shoot date
- [ ] Reference/style research completed and checked against brand guidelines
- [ ] Shoot plan and video concepts/scripts built, covering the full master shot list
- [ ] Shoot scheduled and confirmed with the client the day before
- [ ] Equipment tested and ready
- [ ] Photography and video captured against the plan, with on-site quality checks
- [ ] Consent releases collected and documented on the shoot day for every identifiable subject
- [ ] Final photos and video edited, color-corrected, and exported in every needed format
- [ ] Internal quality and consent-compliance review completed before delivery
- [ ] Final asset library delivered, organized, and confirmed usable by every requesting service
- [ ] Usage and coverage gaps checked monthly for the first quarter after delivery
- [ ] Top-up or refresh shoots scoped efficiently against identified gaps
- [ ] Shoot summary and delivery/consent report completed
- [ ] Asset library reviewed quarterly and refreshed as business details change
